# Sources & figure log

Every number in `index.html` traces to an entry here. **Retrieved September 13, 2026.**

Scope: Maine (Oxford County / South Paris) senior services, MaineCare long-term care,
veteran benefits, and property tax relief. No personal or household details are recorded
in this file.

Almost all of these dollar figures change annually. Re-check the ones marked **ANNUAL** each
January, and the MaineCare/VA figures again each July (the federal spousal-impoverishment
numbers change on a July 1 cycle).

## Home value default

The app ships an **editable placeholder of $275,000** in the house calculator. It is a
starting point for the local market, not a valuation of any particular property, and the app
tells the reader to replace it.

Basis for that figure — ZIP-level market data for 04281 (South Paris):

| Figure | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Median home value, Jun 2026 | $240,400 | [RealtyTrac](https://www.realtytrac.com/market-trends/south-paris-me-04281/) |
| Median sale price | $284,591 | same |
| Reported 12-month change | +10.5% | [WalletInvestor](https://walletinvestor.com/real-estate-forecast/me/oxford/south-paris-housing-market) |

Street-level comparable sales were also reviewed during research and supported the same
range, but are **deliberately not recorded here** — naming individual neighboring properties
would identify the household this guide was originally built for. ZIP-level medians carry the
same information without that.

**To replace the placeholder with a real number:** call the Town of Paris Assessor at
207-743-2501 ext. 4 (assessor@parismaine.org) for the assessed value, and ask any local
realtor for a free market estimate. Assessed value and market value differ — MaineCare and
the VA care about market value.

Note that automated valuation lookup is not practical: Zillow and Redfin block programmatic
retrieval, and the Town of Paris portal
([harris-hopp.com/Maine/Paris/search](https://harris-hopp.com/Maine/Paris/search), linked from
the [town assessing page](https://www.parismaine.org/pages/assessing)) requires an interactive
search.

## Nearby residential care / assisted living

Named in the app from [A Place for Mom — Oxford County](https://www.aplaceformom.com/assisted-living/maine/oxford-county),
[Senior Living Near Me — South Paris](https://www.seniorlivingnearme.org/assisted-living/maine/south-paris.html),
and [Senior Guidance — Oxford County](https://www.seniorguidance.org/senior-housing/maine/oxford-county.html):
Market Square Health Care Center, Paris Home, Elm Hill (South Paris); Norway Center, Spring
Street Home, Beal Street (Norway).

**MaineCare acceptance was NOT verifiable for any of them** and varies by facility and over
time. The app tells the reader to call each one and ask directly — this is deliberate, not an
omission. Care-cost ranges ($4,000–$8,000/mo assisted living, ~$11,000/mo nursing) are
regional estimates for orientation only.
